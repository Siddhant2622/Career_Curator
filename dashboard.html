build How to Fix Formatting Issues

1. Remove invisible tables from Header

In Word/Google Docs, go to View → Show Gridlines to find hidden tables. Delete them and retype the content as plain text.

2. Replace non-standard bullet points

Use standard bullet symbols (• or -). Avoid arrows (→), checkmarks (✓), or emoji in bullet lists.

💡 General Tips

Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri). Avoid text boxes, columns, and images. Save as PDF directly from Word.
