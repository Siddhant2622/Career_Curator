FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS checker and why do I need one? expand_more
An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) checker is a tool that scans your resume to determine how well it will be parsed by the automated recruitment software that over 98% of Fortune 500 companies use. Without proper ATS optimization, your resume might be rejected before a human ever sees it. Career Curator's free ATS checker analyzes your resume for keyword matches, formatting compatibility, and structural issues.
How does the ATS score checker work? expand_more
Our ATS score checker analyzes your resume in four key areas: keyword matching against the target job description, formatting and structural compliance (tables, headers, fonts), section detection (experience, education, skills), and overall readability scoring. You receive a score out of 100 along with specific, actionable recommendations.
Is this ATS resume checker really free? expand_more
Yes! Career Curator's core ATS resume checker is 100% free. Upload your resume, get your ATS compatibility score, keyword analysis, and formatting alerts at no cost. Premium features like AI-powered content suggestions and the optimized resume download can be unlocked by watching a short ad — no payment required.
What file formats are supported? expand_more
Career Curator supports PDF, DOC, and DOCX file formats — the three most common resume file types. We recommend uploading PDFs for the most accurate analysis, as they preserve formatting exactly as intended.
How can I improve my ATS resume score? expand_more
To improve your ATS score: (1) Include keywords from the job description naturally in your experience and skills sections. (2) Use standard section headings like "Experience", "Education", and "Skills". (3) Avoid tables, columns, images, and text boxes. (4) Use standard fonts like Arial or Calibri. (5) Save your resume as a PDF. Our ATS checker gives you specific tips tailored to your resume.
